Our Businesses

Sector-Focused, Globally Integrated

IIC Worldwide operates through a series of defined business verticals, each focused on a specific sector and market opportunity.

Verticals Overview

An Integrated Ecosystem

These verticals are structured to operate independently, with dedicated management, expertise and commercial strategies, while benefiting from the Group's capital strength, governance and global network.

Together, they form an integrated ecosystem that allows IIC Worldwide to participate across the full investment lifecycle — from sourcing and structuring to execution and long-term asset management.

Each vertical is positioned to capture growth within its sector while contributing to the overall strength and diversification of the Group.
