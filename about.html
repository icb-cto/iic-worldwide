Visionary Leadership

Chairman — Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood

Chairman, International Investment Consortium Worldwide (IIC)

Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood is the Founder and Chairman of the International Investment Consortium Inc. (IIC), where he provides the strategic vision that drives the organization’s global growth and diversification. Through his leadership, IIC has evolved into an international platform with interests spanning investment, finance, technology, natural resources, and sustainable development. His commitment to innovation, strategic partnerships, and responsible business practices has strengthened IIC’s presence across multiple regions and industries.

Dato Dr. Arshad holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Economics, a Master’s Degree in Literature, and an Honorary Doctorate in Management Sciences from ACMIT USA. His multidisciplinary academic foundation, combined with decades of business leadership, reflects his dedication to excellence, continuous innovation, and sustainable economic progress.

Guided by an asset-based investment philosophy, Dato Dr. Arshad advocates for creating long-term value through investments backed by tangible assets and sustainable business models. His vision emphasizes responsible growth, resilience, and economic empowerment, enabling IIC to develop impactful ventures that generate lasting value for stakeholders while contributing to the advancement of communities and industries worldwide.

Chairman’s Message

At IIC Worldwide, our mission transcends business — it is about empowering nations and communities through strategic investment, innovation and integrity. We believe that sustainable prosperity requires collaborative action between investors, governments and local stakeholders. As a global consortium, we are committed to responsible investment practices that deliver long-term value while respecting our social and environmental responsibilities.

Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood, Chairman

International Investment Consortium Worldwide (IIC)

Our Identity

A Multinational Investment Consortium

IIC Worldwide is a multinational investment consortium focused on identifying, developing and managing opportunities across critical global sectors.

Headquartered in Dubai, the Group operates through a network of subsidiaries and strategic partnerships, enabling it to engage across both developed and emerging markets. Its portfolio spans traditional industries such as oil and gas, mining and commodities, alongside forward-looking sectors including cryptocurrency, blockchain and digital finance.

At its core, IIC Worldwide is an investment-led organisation with an operational mindset — the Group does not simply deploy capital, it actively shapes, structures and supports projects to ensure long-term viability and measurable returns.

Leadership within IIC Worldwide combines international experience with regional insight, allowing the Group to operate effectively across diverse regulatory environments and market conditions. This approach enables the business to move with agility while maintaining strong governance and compliance standards.

The Group's vision is to build a diversified, future-focused investment platform that supports economic development while delivering sustainable commercial performance.
